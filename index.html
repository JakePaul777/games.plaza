How To Play

Just click on the game icon and it will open up the game in a new tab

How do I close the game?

Press Ctrl+F when a game is open and it will shut down. Feel free to open the site on multiple tabs to have more games running at once.

It's stuck loading/had an error

Attempt to refresh the page by pressing "Ctrl+R" or "Ctrl+Shift+R".
