About BasketBros

Alt link: www.baller.monster. Fun, fast-paced 1 on 1 basketball game with lots of action. Pick from a variety of characters and let the play begin. Go for crazy dunks, hit the stepback 3, or maybe even punch out your opponent!

The controls are very simple: Use either the arrow keys or wasd to control your baller. Jump by pressing the up arrow, and jump again to shoot. Enter/Space will attack if you are on defense.

FAQ

Why isn't the game loading?

You should check your internet connection and make sure our site isn't blocked.

How do I play?

Just hop in and go dunk on some fools!

Which browser should I use?

You should use chrome. It is the only browser guaranteed to work. BasketBros has also been tested to work on FireFox and Safari, but not guaranteed.

Are there more Bros games?

Yes! Our newest is Football Bros, but we have other bros games, including Soccer Bros and Wrestle Bros. Also be sure to check out Hockey Bros!
